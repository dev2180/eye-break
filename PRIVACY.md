# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

Eye Break (also listed in the Microsoft Store as "Eye Break 20-20-20") is an eye-rest app for Windows. This policy
explains what it does with your information. The short version: **it collects nothing about you.**

## What stays on your PC

- **Settings** (break interval, music choice, timer and so on) are saved in `%APPDATA%\EyeBreak\settings.json`.
- **A log** of errors and events is saved in `%APPDATA%\EyeBreak\log.txt`. It is never uploaded. If you choose to
  share it in a bug report, that's up to you.
- The Microsoft Store version keeps both in its own private app storage instead; the panel's **Report** button opens
  the log either way.

Eye Break has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What goes over the internet

Eye Break only goes online for these features:

| When | Service | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Infinite mode music | YouTube (Google) | The video you play or search for, via YouTube's embedded player |
| Quotes, if you choose "ZenQuotes" | ZenQuotes.io | A request for quotes |
| Update checks (installer version only) | GitHub | A request for the latest release |

These services receive your IP address and standard web request information, as any website does. Their own privacy
policies apply: [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [ZenQuotes](https://zenquotes.io/),
[GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children

Eye Break doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
