## Store listing (English)

**Product name:** Eye Break 20-20-20

**Short description** (shown at the top):
Rest your eyes every 20 minutes with a journey past a ray-traced black hole.

**Description:**
Eye Break follows the 20-20-20 rule eye doctors recommend: every 20 minutes, look away from your screen for 20 seconds.

Instead of a nagging pop-up, your eyes gently close over the whole screen, like settling into meditation. You drift past a real-time ray-traced black hole while a ring counts down your rest. A white star is born in the darkness, and a third eye opens you back to your work. Everything you had open keeps running underneath.

When you want a longer rest, Infinite mode lets you float by the black hole with calm music and quotes for as long as you like, with an optional timer that chimes at the start and end.

• A beautiful break every 20 minutes (both adjustable)
• Infinite mode with music, your own YouTube link, and quotes
• Skip breaks for 30 minutes, an hour, two hours, or until you resume
• Covers every monitor, and is hidden from screen sharing and recordings
• Works with fullscreen games
• Locking your PC restarts the cycle, since your eyes are already resting
• Stop a break early any time with Num Lock + numpad 8
• No account, no ads, no tracking

## Submission options: restricted capabilities justification

Paste this when Partner Center asks why the app uses restricted capabilities:

> **runFullTrust:** Eye Break is a desktop (WPF) app. It shows full-screen overlays on every monitor during breaks, uses a low-level keyboard hook during a break so the Windows key and Alt+Tab can't pull other windows over it (typing still reaches the user's apps), and dims the display gamma when a game is in exclusive fullscreen. Breaks can always be skipped from the tray panel, and stopped early with Num Lock + numpad 8. Website: https://github.com/dev2180/eye-break. Support: https://github.com/dev2180/eye-break/issues

(An earlier submission also asked for `unvirtualizedResources`; it was declined and removed in the next package.)
