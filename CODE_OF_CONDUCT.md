# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, welcome people of every background and experience level, give and take feedback
gracefully, and focus on what's best for the people using Eye Break. Harassment, insults, and personal or political
attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project bothers you, report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues, or block anyone who breaks this
code of conduct.
